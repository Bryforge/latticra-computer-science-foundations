# Assignment 01 — Binary and Logic

## Goal

Practice reasoning about binary representation and Boolean logic.

## Part 1 — Binary

Convert the following decimal numbers into binary:

- 1
- 2
- 5
- 8
- 13
- 31
- 64
- 255

Then convert the following binary numbers into decimal:

- `0001`
- `0010`
- `0101`
- `1000`
- `1111`
- `10000000`

## Part 2 — Logic

Create truth tables for:

- `A AND B`
- `A OR B`
- `NOT A`
- `(A AND B) OR C`

## Part 3 — Reflection

Write a short explanation answering:

> Why can simple binary and logic operations support complex computation?

## Completion checklist

- [ ] Decimal-to-binary conversions complete
- [ ] Binary-to-decimal conversions complete
- [ ] Truth tables complete
- [ ] Reflection written
