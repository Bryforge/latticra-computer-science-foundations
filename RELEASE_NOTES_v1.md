## v1 status

This is a complete course foundation. It is suitable for public viewing, self-study, and continued expansion.

The course is not final in the sense of being exhaustive. Future work should deepen examples, add diagrams, add more labs, and expand the programming/security tracks.

## Quality posture

The course intentionally keeps claims grounded. It presents Latticra as a systems design direction and learning context, not as a finished operating system or finished programming language course implementation.
